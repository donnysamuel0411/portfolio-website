About

Designer who asks
why before how.

Jakarta, Indonesia 🇮🇩

I'm a product designer, researcher, and occasional product manager from Indonesia. What started as a curious kid who liked building things has somehow turned into a career I'm genuinely passionate about — which, honestly, feels like cheating.

I've been fortunate to work with companies across wildly different industries, designing digital products that people use every day — from buying groceries to booking a flight. Each project has taught me something new about how humans actually think, which is both endlessly fascinating and occasionally humbling.

My path into UX started during my Computer Science degree at UI, when I realized I was far more interested in the people using the technology than the technology itself. Best pivot I ever made.

What I love most is untangling complex problems and turning them into things that feel obvious in hindsight. When I'm not designing, you'll find me at a pool table, at a concert, or eating something I probably shouldn't have ordered in a city I've never been to before.
